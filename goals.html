My Flight Training Goals

My dream has grown beyond simply becoming a professional pilot. Today, I’m working toward a career in aviation leadership — with the goal of becoming a General Manager of an airport.

I’ve always been drawn to aviation because there is so much more to it than flying. Airports bring together people, aircraft, businesses, communities, and opportunities. I want to be part of the team that makes all of it work

I’m building my career from the ground up, gaining experience in airport operations, customer service, fueling, aviation safety, and the day-to-day realities of a busy airport. Each experience has given me a deeper appreciation for the people behind aviation and the work it takes to keep an airport moving.

While my career is taking me toward airport management, becoming a pilot will always be an important part of my journey. I’m continuing to build my aviation experience and pursue additional ratings, including Instrument, Commercial, and Multi-Engine. I believe that understanding aviation from both the cockpit and the operations side will make me a stronger leader.

Ultimately, I want to help build airports and aviation communities where people can learn, grow, and pursue opportunities they may not have thought were possible. I want to encourage young women and aspiring aviators to see themselves in aviation — whether that means becoming a pilot, mechanic, flight instructor, airport manager, or anything in between.

I don’t know exactly where aviation will take me, but I know I want to be a part of its future.

I’m not just chasing a career in aviation. I’m building a place for myself in it — and hopefully creating room for others to do the same.
